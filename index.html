Why trust the process

A room I never stood in

I asked, in public, for a sound someone remembered that isn't there anymore. One answer:

“I remember the sound of the clock in my grandmothers kitchen. And when I remember that I also recall the way she looked and how her cooking smelled.”

Sixty seconds later there was a room: the clock, the hob, a chair that takes weight, rain on the glass. She wrote back. That is the whole method. One sentence from you, and I build the place it came from.

A loop that clicked, twice

The repair I sell, done on my own material so you can hear it rather than take my word. A 12-second cut joined end to start clicked once per pass: the seam was 10.5 dB harder than the steps the material makes on its own. Fixed without a fade, by finding the join where both ends already sit level and folding half a second across it. After: no step at all, octave bands within 0.2 dB, loudness unmoved at -29.0 LUFS.
