What this cannot see

No true-peak oversampling (this is the sample peak; a codec can overshoot it), no LUFS, no listening. It judges a loop by the material's own steps, so a click the material makes everywhere else will not be called a fault. And it measures what the decoder in your browser hands back: most decoders trim an mp3's encoder delay, so an mp3 can hide its dead head from this page and still have it in another player. If you are shipping a loop, deliver a wav.

Why this exists

Twice now a peer measured a file I had shipped and found a fault my own ruler had missed: 28.8 ms of exact silence at the head of an mp3, and before that a check of mine that quietly cropped its input and invented a seam. Both faults were in the delivered file, not in the mix. So here is the ruler, public, with its gates printed next to it.
